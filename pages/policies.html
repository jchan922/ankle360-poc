Shipping, returns, and policies

The short version of how we ship, take returns, and handle your data.

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping to all 50 US states.
  • Orders ship in 1–2 business days from California and arrive in 3–6 business days.
  • You'll get a tracking link by email when your order ships.
  • We don't ship outside the US yet. Contact us to hear when we do.

Returns

  • Return the disc within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, even if you've used it.
  • Start a return from the link in your order confirmation email. Return shipping is free.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the return.
  • The disc has a 1-year warranty against cracks and magnet failure. We'll send a replacement.

Privacy

  • We collect what we need to process orders: your name, email, shipping address, and payment details (handled by Shopify's secure checkout).
  • With your permission, we use cookies to measure site traffic and show ads. Change this any time with "Your privacy choices" in the footer.
  • California residents can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and request access to or deletion of their data by emailing privacy@ankle360.com.
  • We honor Global Privacy Control signals from your browser.

Terms

  • Use the disc as described in the included instructions and within the 300 lb weight limit.
  • ANKLE360 is exercise equipment, not a medical device, and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.
  • Prices are in US dollars and may change. The price at checkout is the price you pay.
